Privacy notice

Tabi Tabi Po · Salakot Opening Night · Last updated October 2026

Who we are

Flash Packer Inc. (Z Hostel), 5660 Don Pedro Street, Poblacion, Makati, is responsible for the personal data collected on this site. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • RSVP: your name, email, mobile number (optional), how many friends you're bringing, and your answers to the optional questions (costume, drink preference, where you're staying, payment preference, interests, age bracket, notes).
  • At the door: the time your ticket is scanned. Staff check your ID visually; we don't record ID details.
  • How you found us: if you arrived through a promoter or partner link, we record that link's name.
  • Sponsor inquiries: company, contact name, email, mobile (optional), and your message.

Why we use it

  • To put you on the guest list, issue your QR ticket, and check you in at the door.
  • To plan the night: headcount, bar stock, staffing, and the costume contest.
  • To contact you about this event if something changes (for example, a schedule update).
  • Only if you ticked the optional box: to send you news and offers about future Z Hostel and Salakot events.
  • For sponsor inquiries: to reply and discuss a partnership.

We process your data based on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time (see "Your rights").

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not give your personal details to sponsors. Sponsors may receive anonymous totals only (for example, "450 guests attended").

We use these service providers to run the site, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Supabase: stores the guest list (servers in Tokyo, Japan).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website. It doesn't receive your RSVP details.

How long we keep it

We delete RSVP and check-in data 90 days after the event. If you opted in to updates, we keep your name and email for that purpose until you unsubscribe. Sponsor inquiries are kept for up to one year.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you can ask to access, correct, or delete your data, object to its use, and withdraw consent, including unsubscribing from updates. Email us and we'll respond within 15 days. You can also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: marketing@zhostel.com
